# Signage Wayfinding

Owns street signs, regulatory signs, directional signs, maps, storefront sign rules, and wayfinding systems.

Executable seed: `src/generation/public-realm/StreetFurnitureGenerator.ts` creates regulatory signs, street-name signs, and wayfinding signs as `street-furniture` objects with sign-face metadata, curb/sidewalk references, and procedural sign render bindings.
