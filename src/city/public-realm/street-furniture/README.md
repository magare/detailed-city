# Street Furniture

Owns benches, bins, bollards, kiosks, shelters, bike racks, railings, and human-scale props.

Executable seed: `src/generation/public-realm/StreetFurnitureGenerator.ts` creates deterministic detailed-street and citywide benches, bins, bollards, kiosks, bus shelters, bike racks, and railings from curb zones, road profiles, and road-intersection spacing. Objects stay in furnishing zones, carry clear-path, crossing-clearance, visibility, and transit-stop metadata, and bind to procedural street-prop render assets.
