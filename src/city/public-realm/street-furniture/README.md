# Street Furniture

Owns benches, bins, bollards, kiosks, shelters, bike racks, railings, and human-scale props.

Executable seed: `src/generation/public-realm/StreetFurnitureGenerator.ts` creates deterministic detailed-street benches, bins, bollards, kiosks, bus shelters, and bike racks from active curb zones. Objects stay in furnishing zones, carry clearance envelopes, and bind to procedural street-prop render assets.
